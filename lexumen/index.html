Lexumen Daily Crossword

Lexumen is a daily 7×7 crossword built to teach vocabulary rather than test trivia.

Each puzzle is compact enough for a short break, with clue definitions shaped around word meaning instead of celebrity, magazine, or pop-culture knowledge.

Features

  • A new 7×7 puzzle every day.
  • Three difficulty levels: Gentle, Balanced and Challenge.
  • Hints earned by solving answers unaided.
  • One-time unlock for the full experience, not a subscription.
  • Plays offline.
